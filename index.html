About Us

Established in 2025 with a vision to redefine the boundaries between form and function, Klee Studio draws inspiration from basic geometric shapes and primary colors.

While the studio itself is a fresh endeavor, our founder brings deep industry expertise, having actively worked and innovated in the interior architecture field since 2017. We believe that every building and interior space should tell the story of its inhabitants while responding harmoniously to its environment.
